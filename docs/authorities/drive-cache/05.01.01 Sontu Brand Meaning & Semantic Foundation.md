Sontu Brand Meaning & Semantic Foundation  
Status: Founder-approved canonical branding foundation  
Locked: September 6, 2026

PURPOSE  
This document governs the semantic foundation used to develop Sontu’s brand identity, logo, emblem, wordmark, visual language, messaging, and future brand lore. The meanings below are complementary layers; Sontu is a coined international identity and should not be presented as though it has one literal Vietnamese dictionary definition.

CORE IDENTITY  
Primary international identity: Sontu / SONTU  
Secondary Vietnamese identity: Sốntụ

MEANING LAYERS

1\. Sống — life / living  
Sống provides the foundational idea of being alive and actually living one’s life. Within the Sontu brand, it represents lived experience rather than passive observation.

2\. Tụ — gathering / convergence  
Tụ carries the idea of gathering, congregating, converging, or coming together. Sốntụ is a coined construction, not an established Vietnamese compound. Its intended poetic readings include:  
• Life comes together.  
• People come together to live.  
• Living through gathering.

3\. Sống² — Live² lineage  
Sống² inherits the original philosophy of Live². The superscript is not primarily a mathematical claim. It holds two senses of “live” simultaneously:  
• LIVE — to live one’s life.  
• LIVE — happening now; being present in the moment.  
Canonical philosophical reading: Live life. Live now.  
Sống² may additionally suggest living more fully or life amplified, but that is secondary to the dual “live” meaning.

4\. Sơn — mountain  
Sơn is the Sino-Vietnamese reading associated with mountain. As a secondary phonetic/cultural layer within Sontu, it introduces imagery of mountains and the physical world. Symbolic associations such as journey, ascent, permanence, destination, perspective, and real places may inform branding, but they are symbolic extensions rather than literal definitions of Sontu.

5\. Tú — beauty / elegance / excellence  
Tú can carry Sino-Vietnamese meanings associated with beauty, elegance, luxuriance, distinction, or excellence depending on the underlying character and naming context.

6\. Sơn Tú — landscape / mountain-beauty layer  
The phonetic reading Sơn \+ Tú creates a secondary cultural layer that can evoke beautiful mountains, natural beauty, landscape, elevation, and the world beyond the screen. This is distinct from the coined Sống \+ tụ construction.

7\. Sontu — international coined identity  
Sontu intentionally works internationally without requiring users to understand Vietnamese. Removing Vietnamese diacritics allows multiple Vietnamese echoes to coexist within a short coined identity. Public branding does not need to explain every layer; deeper interpretations can remain brand lore.

PRODUCT MEANING  
Sontu is not fundamentally about managing events or maximizing digital engagement. Its deeper product movement is:  
Possibility → credible information → commitment → preparation → convergence → shared lived moment → continuity.

Sontu should optimize successful real-world participation. The product can be successful when someone spends relatively little time in it but discovers, prepares for, and actually experiences something worthwhile.

The shared event can be understood as the temporary convergence of separate people, intentions, schedules, places, information, and relationships around a common place \+ time \+ purpose. Afterward, people separate again, while useful relationships, memories, knowledge, and host learning may continue.

Canonical conceptual pattern:  
Separate → converge → share → diverge → continue.

HOST \+ PARTICIPANT LAYER  
For participants, Sontu helps turn possibility and intent into informed real-world participation.  
For hosts, Sontu helps turn observed event outcomes into better next-event decisions.  
The event is the shared centre where those two perspectives meet.

BRAND PHILOSOPHY  
The strongest consolidated philosophical territory is:  
LIFE \+ NOW \+ TOGETHER \+ WORLD.

Supporting interpretation:  
Sontu helps people turn possibilities into experiences they actually live.

Deeper brand-lore interpretation:  
Live life. Live now. Come together. Experience the world.

This language is a semantic foundation, not a locked public tagline unless separately approved.

VISUAL IMPLICATIONS  
Future Sontu identity exploration should prioritize abstract concepts such as:  
• convergence;  
• a shared centre or shared moment;  
• separate paths becoming one experience;  
• continuity after the moment;  
• life, motion, and presence;  
• horizon, mountain, place, or the physical world;  
• multiple independent identities temporarily gathering without losing individuality.

The emblem should ideally support more than one reading, reflecting the layered nature of the name itself.

Avoid defaulting to generic event-category symbols such as calendars, tickets, location pins, confetti, balloons, literal people silhouettes, hearts, leaves, or generic community circles. A Sontu mark should represent the philosophy or moment of convergence rather than merely advertise “event app.”

BRAND-GOVERNANCE CAUTION  
Preserve Vietnamese diacritics when documenting the linguistic sources: Sống, tụ, Sơn, and Tú are distinct readings. Sontu is the international coined brand and must not be falsely represented as a Vietnamese dictionary word with a single literal definition. Linguistic meanings, intended wordplay, symbolic associations, and product-derived interpretations should remain clearly distinguished.

APPROVAL  
Founder-approved and locked as the current semantic foundation for Sontu branding. Future logo and identity work should derive from this foundation unless the founder explicitly revises it.

BRAND IDENTITY DECISION — WORKING LOCK, SEPTEMBER 12, 2026

Emblem direction  
Working lock: V6 Minimal / selected four-part convergence geometry. The emblem consists of four independent rounded forms arranged around an open shared centre, visually expressing separate identities/paths converging around a common moment while retaining individuality. Do not casually alter the geometry during downstream UI design. Formal vector reconstruction, optical balancing and trademark/design clearance may refine execution without changing the governing concept.

Primary color direction  
Working lock: Classic Ocean. The selected identity uses a cool ocean-blue family ranging from light/cyan blue through saturated blue to deep navy. Exact production color values require vector/color verification before final asset export; image-generation approximations are not authoritative color specifications.

Wordmark  
Current Sontu wordmark direction is retained as a strong working candidate. Refine rather than replace by default. Any custom letter treatment should remain subtle, legible internationally and subordinate to the emblem rather than competing with it.

Leading tagline candidate  
Living life, together.

Rationale: the phrase directly reinforces the brand’s Sống \+ tụ semantic foundation — life/living \+ gathering/together — while also preserving the original Live² lineage of actively living rather than passively observing. It is broad enough to survive expansion beyond conventional event management into real-world experiences, communities, places and participation.

Tagline status and collision caution  
The phrase is a leading brand-language candidate, not represented as legally exclusive or trademark-cleared. Preliminary web review found widespread descriptive/editorial use of “living life together” and related formulations, including religious/community contexts, podcasts, institutional writing and a 2025 U.S. application for the longer mark “WINNIE\! LIVING LIFE TOGETHER” in entertainment services. This suggests the phrase itself is semantically common and likely weaker as a standalone exclusive identifier. Its value to Sontu is therefore primarily as supporting brand language used with the distinctive Sontu name and emblem. Formal trademark counsel/search is required before any claim of exclusivity or registration strategy.

Brand hierarchy  
Primary identifier: Sontu / SONTU.  
Primary visual identifier: V6 four-part convergence emblem.  
Primary color direction: Classic Ocean.  
Leading supporting tagline: Living life, together.  
Deeper philosophy remains: Live life. Live now. Come together. Experience the world.

Governance  
Detailed Sontu UI design should now derive from this identity direction rather than placeholder emblems or unrelated generated marks. The emblem, wordmark and tagline should not be fused into every product surface; use brand assets with restraint so the experience itself remains primary.

PRODUCTION IDENTITY CHECKPOINT — September 12, 2026

Status: Founder-selected identity direction; production reconstruction required before final asset export.

Emblem  
The selected emblem direction is the V6 Minimal four-part convergence geometry, specifically the more-spaced geometry selected during visual review. Preserve four independent curved forms around an open shared centre. The forms should read as separate paths/identities participating in one coherent rotational/convergent system. Do not revert to the denser A2 flower, generic ring, horizon, star, or S-path alternatives.

Geometry requirements for production reconstruction:  
• Four rotationally balanced forms at 90-degree intervals.  
• Open central negative space remains visually dominant and clean at small sizes.  
• Forms do not physically merge into one solid mass.  
• Outer silhouette remains approximately circular without becoming a literal ring.  
• Curvature should feel continuous and directional rather than petal-like or floral.  
• Minimum-size test must remain recognizable at 16–24 px.  
• A one-color silhouette must remain coherent without relying on gradients.  
• Do not stretch, rotate individual forms independently, add shadows/outlines, or alter geometry between color themes.

Color direction  
Classic Ocean is the selected primary color direction. The current production-candidate anchors are:  
• Ocean Light: \#38BDF8  
• Ocean: \#0EA5E9  
• Ocean Deep: \#2563EB  
• Navy: \#0F2D5B

These values are now the working production candidate and should be tested for accessibility, display consistency, print conversion and dark/light-background behavior before final immutable brand lock. The emblem may use a controlled multi-stop gradient derived from these anchors. Monochrome, solid Navy, black and white/knockout variants are required.

Wordmark  
Retain the current rounded modern lowercase “Sontu” direction. Refinement should be subtle and proprietary rather than decorative. Explore small custom drawing/spacing adjustments, especially around the o/n/t relationships, while preserving immediate legibility. The emblem remains the primary distinctive device; the wordmark should complement rather than compete with it.

Tagline  
Leading public tagline candidate: “Living life, together.”  
Use sentence case. The comma is intentional because it separates the lived-life idea from the together/convergence idea. The tagline is supporting messaging and should not be fused permanently into the emblem geometry. Logo assets must exist both with and without tagline.

Required production asset families  
• Emblem only — gradient/color.  
• Emblem only — solid Navy.  
• Emblem only — black.  
• Emblem only — white/knockout.  
• Horizontal emblem \+ wordmark.  
• Stacked emblem \+ wordmark.  
• Horizontal and stacked lockups with optional tagline.  
• Light-background and dark-background versions.  
• App icon/master icon derived from the same emblem geometry.  
• Favicon/small-size variants that preserve the same identity rather than introducing a different mark.

Production rule  
AI-generated boards are visual references, not source artwork. Final implementation must use reconstructed vector geometry and verified tokens/assets. Do not trace incidental rendering artifacts, gradients, spacing, or typography errors from generated boards into production authority.

BRAND APPLICATION HIERARCHY — FOUNDER DIRECTION  
Added September 12, 2026

Primary day-to-day brand identifier: Sontu wordmark.  
The wordmark should be the dominant brand signature across product UI, website/product chrome, communications and ordinary branded contexts where the name can be displayed clearly.

Emblem role: secondary/symbolic identifier. Reserve the V6 convergence emblem primarily for contexts where a compact symbol is functionally or culturally stronger than the full wordmark, including app icon, favicon, avatar/social icon, apparel, merchandise, event/environmental applications, badges, small-format marks and selected brand moments.

Do not mechanically place the emblem beside the wordmark throughout the product. A combined emblem \+ wordmark lockup remains available when a formal brand lockup is useful, but the default product-brand treatment should prefer the wordmark alone. The emblem may also appear independently where brand recognition has sufficient context.

This hierarchy preserves the emblem as a distinctive asset rather than overusing it as decorative UI. Product navigation and controls must not use the emblem as a substitute for universal functional icons.
